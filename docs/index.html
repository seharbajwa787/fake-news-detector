📰 Fake News Detector

Paste a news headline or article below. Runs entirely in your browser.

This is a lightweight, browser-only version of a full Python model trained on 4,594 real news articles (2016 US political news). The full model reaches ~94% accuracy; this in-browser version (using the top 350 most predictive words) runs at ~85% accuracy so it can work instantly with no server. It's strongest on political/election-style text, since that's what it learned from.
